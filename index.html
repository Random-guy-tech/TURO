Know what you’re getting before you pay.

  1. Tell us what you’re looking for

    Send the course or book title, its creator, or a link. A specific title helps us check the right material.

  2. Confirm availability and price

    We check the material first, then confirm the price, delivery timing, and payment details. If it isn’t available, we’ll ask you to wait until we can source it.

  3. Pay, then receive your download

    Once availability is confirmed and payment is arranged, we send a link to a file-hosting website where you can download your material.

Crypto is our preferred payment method. Bank transfer is also available. Confirm the total and payment details with us on Telegram before sending funds.

How will I receive the course or book?

We send you a download link on a file-hosting website. You download the supplied material from there. Confirm the exact contents with us for your chosen title before payment.

How long does delivery take?

We confirm the delivery timing for your request on Telegram before payment. If a title is unavailable, we’ll let you know and ask you to wait until we can get it. We check availability before asking you to pay.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We mainly accept cryptocurrency. Bank transfer is also available. We’ll confirm the payment method and exact amount with you on Telegram after checking availability.

Can I get a refund?

If we cannot supply the requested material after you have paid, we’ll refund your payment. Once the correct course or book you requested has been delivered, the purchase is non-refundable. We check availability before asking for payment.

What if I need help after buying?

Contact us on Telegram at @ibsaks for help with your request or download. Support is available 24/7. Keep your conversation and payment details so we can identify your order.
